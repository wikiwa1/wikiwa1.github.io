Popa's relative dimension set $$\Lambda(M,N)$$ of an inclusion of II$$_1$$ factors $$N\subset M$$ consists of the scalars $$\alpha$$ for which some projection $$p\in M$$ satisfies $$E_N(p)=\alpha 1$$. Popa computed it for index at most $$4$$, and for index $$d>4$$ outside the middle interval $$(t_d,1-t_d)$$, where $$t_d(1-t_d)=d^{-1}$$; whether the middle interval meets $$\Lambda(M,N)$$ for every irreducible inclusion of index $$d>4$$ has remained open. We show that for every extremal inclusion of finite index $$d>4$$, the whole interval $$[t_d,1-t_d]$$ is contained in $$\Lambda(M,N)$$. Consequently, $$\Lambda(M,N)$$ depends only on the index for all extremal inclusions of finite index, in particular for all irreducible ones. Extremality cannot be dropped: for $$d>4$$, Popa's locally trivial inclusions are exactly those whose relative dimension set misses the middle interval. Other non-extremal inclusions can contain the whole middle interval in their relative dimension set, or miss part of it.
