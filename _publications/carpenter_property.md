We show that if $$N\subset M$$ is an inclusion of II$$_1$$ factors of uniform infinite index, for instance an irreducible inclusion of infinite index, then every positive contraction $$b\in N$$ is of the form $$E_N(p)$$ for a projection $$p\in M$$. This gives the exact version of Popa's asymptotic result in the ultrapower, and it is a subfactor analogue of the Carpenter problem for masas. In particular, Popa's relative dimension set of such an inclusion is $$[0,1]$$, so the set of expectations of projections does not distinguish inclusions without corners of finite index. As applications, the Carpenter property holds for $$N\subset N\rtimes G$$ whenever an infinite group $$G$$ acts outerly on a II$$_1$$ factor $$N$$, and for ICC groups $$H\le G$$ the inclusion $$L(H)\subset L(G)$$ has it exactly when $$[G:H]=\infty$$, confirming a recent conjecture. We also give examples showing that the Carpenter property does not imply uniform infinite index, and that infinite index does not imply the Carpenter property.
