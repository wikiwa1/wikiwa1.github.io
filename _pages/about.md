I am currently a postdoctoral scholar at Vanderbilt University working under the supervision of Dietmar Bisch. My main research interests are quantum symmetries, with an emphasis on those that can be captured via operator algebras or fusion categories.

With Dietmar Bisch, I have constructed new hyperfinite subfactors with infinite depth from commuting squares. More recently, I computed Popa's relative dimension set for every extremal subfactor of finite index, and with Bisch I proved the Carpenter property for subfactors of uniform infinite index.
